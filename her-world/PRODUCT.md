## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Create fictional AI characters, direct generated video shows, prepare associated Pump coin launches, and operate a supervised browser broadcast studio. This redesign is a local review copy of the latest repository, focused on exposing launchpad capabilities clearly.

## Users

Character creators editing a character and its show; visitors browsing confirmed tokens. These roles are evidenced by the existing routes and server ownership checks. Further audience positioning is open.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing app includes wallet-owned drafts, device drafts, reference images, personality and voice selection, scene editing, separate coin artwork, AI scripts, durable image/video jobs, reviewed coin creation, a token directory and per-character broadcast studios. Generation, database access and coin submission require configured services and wallet ownership. Local review uses no production credentials. Never represent concept artwork, mock data, a prepared transaction or transport acceptance as an actual launch or verified live stream.

## Brand Commitments

Keep ACP / Artificial Character Protocol and its existing HER collective. The user chose a dark creator studio, rejected decorative human portraits and the signal-green palette, and requests a simpler shadcn interface. The first angular ACP mark was rejected. Use a refined generated mark, a dimensional pearl/silver hero, and a small family of companion backgrounds placed selectively across the site; keep controls quiet and the overall result clean. Four photographic setting choices remain. The UI exposes four steps, PFP-only coin artwork, and fixed ten-minute preparation with no buffer control. The homepage demonstrates editable creator controls. Preserve the product's actual workflows and provider gates. No decorative fictional people in the interface.

## Evidence on Hand

Repository at df39b72; current ACP-LAUNCHPAD.md and implementation routes. Recorded pilot results are historical evidence, not live preview results. There are no supplied testimonials or reliable platform-wide metrics.

## Product Principles

- Keep character references distinct from token branding.
